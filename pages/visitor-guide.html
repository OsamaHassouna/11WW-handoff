Cultural & Religious Activities

Umrah Arrangements

  1. Available through accredited agencies near the venue
  2. Recommended to plan in advance due to high season
  3. Valid Umrah visa required for non-Saudi residents

Cultural Tours & Experiences

  1. Historic Al-Balad District (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  2. Jeddah Corniche Waterfront
  3. Al Rahma Mosque (Floating Mosque)
  4. King Fahd Fountain (tallest in the world)
  5. Al Tayebat International City Museum
  6. Red Sea Mall & Jeddah Mall
  7. Traditional souqs (Al-Balad Souq, Gold Souq)
