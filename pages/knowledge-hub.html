Water Security in Arid Regions: Saudi Arabia Case Study

Case Study Water Security

This comprehensive case study examines Saudi Arabia's innovative approaches to water security in arid conditions. The research covers desalination technologies, water recycling systems, smart irrigation practices, and policy frameworks that have enabled the Kingdom to achieve water security despite limited freshwater resources. Key findings include the importance of integrated water resource management, public-private partnerships in infrastructure development, and community engagement in water conservation efforts.
