Event Venue

The Ritz-Carlton, Jeddah

Jeddah Corniche, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

The 2nd Stakeholder Consultation Meeting will take place at The Ritz-Carlton, Jeddah, one of the most distinguished venues in the region. Located on the Corniche overlooking the Red Sea, the hotel provides an exceptional setting for high-level dialogue and international collaboration.

The property offers grand meeting halls, secure and fully equipped conference rooms, and premium hospitality services suitable for ministerial, technical, and multi-stakeholder discussions.

Participants will benefit from spacious facilities, advanced audiovisual capabilities, dedicated breakout areas, and on-site support teams that ensure smooth and efficient meeting operations. The venue's proximity to the city's main access points, hotels, and coastal landmarks makes it convenient for both local and international delegates.
