Youth Program

A platform for youth participation, contribution, and representation across the 11th World Water Forum and its Processes, turning intergenerational dialogue into lasting action for Riyadh 2027.

About the Program

Young people are key stakeholders in achieving a water-secure future, as influencers, innovators, entrepreneurs, researchers, and community leaders.

Guided by the theme Action for a Better Tomorrow, the 11th World Water Forum is institutionalizing youth participation through a dedicated Youth Program, led by the Riyadh Water Youth Committee, which is the official youth engagement mechanism of the 11th Forum.

Program Objectives

Meaningful participation

Ensure inclusive youth participation and contribution across the thematic, regional, and political processes of the Forum.

Lasting impact

Create lasting action and on-the-ground impact through a well-coordinated approach that extends beyond the Forum.

Intergenerational dialogue

Strengthen dialogue and collaboration between youth, governments, international organizations, academia, civil society, and the private sector.

Youth-led solutions

Elevate youth-led solutions and innovations that contribute to water security, climate resilience, and ecosystem protection.

Actionable contributions

Translate youth knowledge, experiences, and recommendations into contributions that inform Forum discussions and outcomes.

Global network

Foster a global network of youth stakeholders committed to advancing water action beyond the Forum.

Legacy framework

Create continuity between successive World Water Forums, contributing to ongoing global water processes.

Core Program Components

Youth Consultations

Regional and global consultations enabling young people to contribute priorities, recommendations, and perspectives to Forum discussions.

Cross-Process Integration

Structured engagement with the thematic, regional, and political processes to ensure youth contributions are reflected across Forum outcomes.

Intergenerational Dialogue

Exchanges between youth representatives and senior decision-makers to promote mutual learning, collaboration, and action.

Capacity Building & Knowledge Sharing

Activities to strengthen youth leadership, technical knowledge, and participation in water governance.

Outreach Activities

Mobilization, awareness campaigns, digital engagement, and storytelling through film, photography, design, and other creative platforms.

Follow-Up Actions

Documentation of outcomes, recommendations, commitments, and legacy initiatives beyond the Forum.
